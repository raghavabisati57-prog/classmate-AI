# ClassMate AI
### Missed a Class? Never Miss Learning.

## Overview
ClassMate AI is an AI-powered educational platform designed to help students catch up on missed lectures, understand difficult concepts, and organize their learning activities in one place.

The project addresses a common challenge in education: students who miss classes may struggle to access learning materials, clarify doubts, and keep up with their coursework.

## Problem Statement
When students miss lectures, they may miss important explanations and learning activities. Finding relevant notes, getting answers to questions, and planning how to catch up can take additional time and effort.

There is a need for a simple platform that brings lecture access, question answering, and catch-up planning together.

## Our Solution
ClassMate AI provides a centralized learning experience where faculty can share lectures, students can access available learning materials, an AI Tutor can explain concepts, and a catch-up planner can help students track their learning tasks.

## Key Features

### 1. Faculty Desk
Allows faculty members to post lecture information and share learning content with students.

### 2. Lectures Feed
Allows students to browse available lectures and find topics they need to study.

### 3. AI Tutor
Uses Gemini-powered AI to answer student questions and explain concepts in simple language.

### 4. Catch-up Planner
Helps students organize missed topics, plan their learning activities, and track task completion.

### 5. Student View
Provides students with access to the learning features in a single interface.

## Inspiration
The idea came from a common student challenge: missing a lecture can make it harder to understand a topic and keep up with coursework. Students may need to search for notes, contact classmates, and independently organize their study plans.

We wanted to simplify this process by bringing lecture sharing, AI-powered explanations, and learning task management into one platform.

## How It Works
1. Faculty members post lecture information.
2. Students browse the available lectures.
3. Students ask the AI Tutor questions about the topics they are studying.
4. Students organize their catch-up activities using the planner.
5. Students track their progress as they complete learning tasks.

## Technology Stack
- Google AI Studio — AI-assisted application development
- React and TypeScript — application interface and logic
- Vite — frontend development and build tooling
- Gemini API — AI-powered question answering

## What Makes ClassMate AI Useful?
- Brings important learning tools into one platform.
- Helps students clarify doubts through AI-powered explanations.
- Supports more organized self-paced learning.
- Helps students plan and track their catch-up activities.
- Provides a foundation for future personalized learning features.

## Future Improvements
- Automatic lecture summaries and notes.
- Practice quizzes and topic-based assessments.
- Personalized study recommendations.
- Secure college or student account integration.

## Current Scope and Limitations
ClassMate AI is a working prototype demonstrating lecture sharing, AI-assisted question answering, and study planning. The accuracy of AI responses depends on the question, available context, and AI service availability. Further testing, secure user authentication, and persistent data storage can be added as the project develops.

## Conclusion
ClassMate AI aims to make missed lectures easier to recover from by combining lecture access, AI-assisted learning, and study planning in one student-friendly platform.
